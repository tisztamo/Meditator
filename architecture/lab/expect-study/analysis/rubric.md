Checkable claim
  A concrete, falsifiable statement about what the consequence will show
  (a number, a title, a file existing, a specific phrase on the screen).

Restated intent
  The expect text restates the reach gist ("I will look it up") rather than
  predicting the world's answer.

Vague prose
  Mood, hope, or uncheckable quality ("it will go well", "something useful").

Other
  Empty after trim, a copy of the hand arguments, or anything that does not
  fit the three above.
